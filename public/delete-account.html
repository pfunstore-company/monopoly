刪除 Boolionaire 帳號

Delete your Boolionaire account

刪除後，帳號及雲端遊戲進度將永久消失，無法復原。
This permanently deletes your account and cloud progress. It cannot be undone.

你也可以在遊戲內「設定」→「刪除帳號」完成。
You can also do this in the game: Settings → Delete account.

或者用舊帳號名稱和密碼 / or with an old account name and password:

Apple 帳號請在 iPhone 遊戲內「設定」→「刪除帳號」。
For a Sign in with Apple account, use Settings → Delete account in the iPhone game.
